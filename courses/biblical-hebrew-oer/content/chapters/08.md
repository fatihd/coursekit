# Unit 8 — Roots and Patterns

Hebrew words can be organized by consonantal roots and recurring word patterns. This unit introduces the analytical method and its limits.

## Summary

Many Hebrew roots contain three consonants. A pattern places vowels and sometimes additional consonants around the root to form a noun, adjective, or verb. Several words may share a root but differ in grammatical form and meaning. Root analysis helps organize vocabulary; it does not reliably predict an unfamiliar word's meaning, so confirm each form in a lexicon.
