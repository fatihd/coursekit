# Unit 4 — Masoretic Pointing and Vowel Signs

The Masoretic pointing system records vowels and other reading information alongside the consonants. This unit introduces how vowel points are positioned and named.

## Summary

Vowel signs may appear above, below, or inside a consonant. Hebrew has five basic vowel qualities—i, e, a, o, and u—and more than one sign can represent a quality. Long and short vowels are distinguished through a combination of sign, syllable structure, stress, and spelling. First recognize the point, then read the full syllable rather than assuming that one mark always has one fixed English sound.
