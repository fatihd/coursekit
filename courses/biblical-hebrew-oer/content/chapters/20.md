# Unit 20 — Hollow and Lamed-He Verbs; Review

Hollow roots have a weak middle radical; Lamed-He roots have a weak final radical. This final unit consolidates the strong and weak patterns introduced in the course.

## Summary

In hollow verbs, middle waw or yod often appears as a long vowel in finite forms. In Lamed-He verbs, final he or yod can change or disappear when endings are added. Compare the Qatal, Yiqtol, participle, and tenseless forms of each root class. For a new verb, identify the root, locate the stem and conjugation markers, and use a lexicon to confirm its meaning. This is a strategy for continuing study, not a substitute for reading verbs in context.
