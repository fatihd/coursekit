# Unit 14 — Pronominal Suffixes and Other Prepositions

Hebrew attaches pronominal suffixes to nouns and prepositions. Other prepositions are written separately or joined by maqqef.

## Summary

A suffix on a noun commonly expresses possession; on a preposition it identifies the preposition's object. Suffix forms vary by person, number, and host word, so compare examples instead of memorizing one isolated list. The preposition מִן often changes before its host. אֵת commonly marks a definite direct object and usually has no separate English translation.
