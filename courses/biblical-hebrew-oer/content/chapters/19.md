# Unit 19 — Weak Verbs: Pe-Nun and Pe-Yod

Weak verbs contain a root consonant that changes or disappears in certain patterns. This unit begins with roots whose first radical is nun or yod.

## Summary

When a prefix places the first radical directly beside the second, a weak initial consonant may assimilate or change. In Pe-Nun verbs, nun can disappear before a consonant that takes dagesh; before letters that resist doubling, it may remain visible. Pe-Yod verbs show changes in several Qal forms. Identify the root class first, then compare forms across the paradigm rather than treating each surface change as a separate lexeme.
