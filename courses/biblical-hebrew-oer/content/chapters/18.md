# Unit 18 — Imperative, Jussive, Cohortative, and Infinitives

These forms express commands, wishes, intentions, and actions treated as nouns. Their subject and clause roles distinguish them from ordinary finite statements.

## Summary

An imperative normally addresses the listener. A jussive expresses a desired or directed action for a third person, while a cohortative presents the speaker's or group's intention. The infinitive construct can function as a noun or form a subordinate clause and is often translated with “to.” The infinitive absolute can emphasize an action or serve other constructions. Use the form and clause context together to select a translation.
