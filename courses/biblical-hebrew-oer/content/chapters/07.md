# Unit 7 — Dagesh and Meteg

Dagesh marks either pronunciation or consonant doubling. Meteg provides another clue for reading a pointed word.

## Summary

Dagesh qal occurs in a Begadkephat letter and often marks its stop pronunciation. Dagesh hazaq marks a doubled consonant and can appear in many other letters, though gutturals and resh resist doubling. Meteg is a short vertical mark beneath a letter and can support syllable or stress interpretation. Identify the letter class and syllable position before deciding what a dagesh means.
