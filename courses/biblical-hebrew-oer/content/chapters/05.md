# Unit 5 — Vowel Qualities

This unit compares full and reduced vowel signs and relates them to the spellings introduced earlier.

## Summary

Pataḥ and qamats commonly represent an a quality; segol and tsere represent e; hiriq represents i; holam and qamats qatan represent o; and qibbuts or shureq represent u. Reduced vowels include shewa and the three ḥataf signs. The written sign, syllable, and stress jointly help determine a vowel's reading. Learn common combinations as patterns, while remembering that spelling conventions vary by word.
