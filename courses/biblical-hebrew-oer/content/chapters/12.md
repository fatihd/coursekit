# Unit 12 — Demonstratives and Waw

Demonstratives identify a referent as “this” or “that.” The conjunction waw attaches to a word and links phrases or clauses.

## Summary

Demonstrative forms agree with nouns in gender and number, and they can appear alone or next to a noun. Waw commonly means “and,” but it can also introduce contrast, consequence, or a main clause after a dependent clause. Its vowels change in some environments. Identify waw as a prefix, then choose its English function from the relationship between the linked clauses.
