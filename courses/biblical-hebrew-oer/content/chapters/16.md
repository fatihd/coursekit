# Unit 16 — The Verb Stems

Hebrew uses several stems built from a root. This unit compares their common form markers and semantic tendencies.

## Summary

The major stems include Qal, Nifal, Piel, Pual, Hitpael, Hifil, and Hofal. Stem changes can affect voice, valency, or lexical meaning. Nifal often has passive or middle uses; Piel and Pual frequently form an active/passive pair; Hitpael often has reflexive or reciprocal uses; Hifil and Hofal commonly express causative and passive-causative relations. These are tendencies, not translation rules; verify each verb in a lexicon.
