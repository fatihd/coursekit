# Unit 17 — Yiqtol and Tenseless Forms

Yiqtol uses prefixes to mark person. Some related forms express possibility, desire, command, or verbal action without a fixed time reference.

## Summary

Yiqtol has a prefix in every person and suffixes in some gender-and-number forms. It can express future, repeated, general, or modal meanings, so it is not equivalent to one English tense. Some forms are identical and must be interpreted from context. Imperatives, jussives, cohortatives, and infinitives share parts of the verbal system but have distinct clause roles.
