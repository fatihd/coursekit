# Unit 13 — Prefix Prepositions

Three common prepositions attach directly to the following word. Their surface form changes in predictable sound environments.

## Summary

The prefixes בְּ, כְּ, and לְ express several relations, including location, comparison, and direction. Because they attach to the host, read them as part of the written word while identifying their grammatical role. Before the definite article or a shewa, their vowels may change. A prefix's English translation depends on its phrase and context rather than a single fixed gloss.
