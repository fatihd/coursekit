# Unit 10 — Independent Pronouns

Independent personal pronouns are separate words. Their forms identify person and number, and the second and third persons also distinguish gender.

## Summary

First person refers to the speaker, second person to the addressee, and third person to someone or something being discussed. First-person pronouns are common gender; second- and third-person forms distinguish masculine and feminine. Hebrew verbs often mark the subject themselves, so an independent pronoun can add emphasis or contrast.

## Pronoun Paradigm

| Person | Singular | Plural |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1st common | אֲנִי | אֲנַחְנוּ |
| 2nd masculine | אַתָּה | אַתֶּם |
| 2nd feminine | אַתְּ | אַתֵּן |
| 3rd masculine | הוּא | הֵם |
| 3rd feminine | הִיא | הֵן |
