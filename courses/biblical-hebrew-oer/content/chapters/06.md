# Unit 6 — Syllables and Shewa

Word reading becomes more reliable when vowel signs are grouped into syllables. This unit distinguishes open and closed syllables and the two functions of shewa.

## Summary

An open syllable ends in a vowel; a closed syllable ends in a consonant. Vocal shewa begins a syllable with a brief vowel, while silent shewa marks a consonant at the end of a syllable. The same written mark can play either role. Use its position, neighboring vowels, and stress as clues, and check the whole word when the pattern is irregular.
