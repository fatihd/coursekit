# Content attribution and license

This course's lesson text and vocabulary draw on public-domain Hebrew grammars and lexica,
so they are free of the copyright and source-derived restrictions that apply to the
authoring hub's other courses.

## Provenance of the lesson text

The lesson prose (`content/chapters/*.md`) is **newly written for this course** and is **not**
a transcription or close adaptation of any grammar's actual wording. The unit sequence and
topic coverage are **informed by** the public-domain grammar below, but the sentences,
examples, and explanations were written fresh. No source text was copied; there is no
verbatim Davidson text in this repository.

The lesson prose was produced with machine assistance and has **not yet received human
Hebrew review**; that review and editing is a release gate.

## Sequence and coverage reference

- **A. B. Davidson,** *An Introductory Hebrew Grammar, with Progressive Exercises in
  Reading, Writing, and Pointing* (22nd edition, revised by John Edgar McFadyen,
  Edinburgh: T. & T. Clark, 1923). Public domain in the United States (published before
  1931) and in life-plus-70 jurisdictions (Davidson d. 1902, McFadyen d. 1933). It is the
  course's **topic sequence** reference; the § numbers cited in each lesson's Source line refer
  to this edition (ADR-021). Its §1–§49 numbering and section titles are the same as in the
  19th edition (1914) the course was first planned from.
- **John Edgar McFadyen,** *Key to the Exercises in the late Professor A. B. Davidson's
  revised Introductory Hebrew Grammar* (1924). Public domain (US pre-1931; McFadyen d. 1933). It
  answers the 22nd edition's exercises. The course drills (`authored/`) are the grammar's
  exercises §6, §8, §11–§49 with the key's answers, transcribed by hand from the scans in the
  Exercise Workbench (`exwb/curated/`, CC0 transcription) and generated into the course by
  `build/exwb_to_authored.py`. Every item links back to its prompt and answer scan pages
  (`source.promptUrl` / `source.answerUrl`) and cites its § and item number. The reading and
  transliteration drills (§1–§5, §7, §9) are held for now (`exwb/EXEMPTIONS.md`). See `build/_sources/davidson/SOURCES.md` and ADR-017 (AGENTS.md §12).
- Grammar (22nd ed.): <https://archive.org/details/introductoryhebr0000davi_w1b2>
- Key: <https://archive.org/details/keytoexercisesin0000mcfa>
- 19th edition (1914), historical: <https://archive.org/details/introductoryheb00daviuoft>
- Edition-compatibility audit and local source checksums: `build/_sources/davidson/SOURCES.md`

## Vocabulary provenance

Word forms and glosses are standard Biblical Hebrew headwords and dictionary senses; they
are not copyrightable expression. Glosses, roots, and Strong's references are cross-checked
against Brown–Driver–Briggs and Strong's (both public domain). Frequency values are token
occurrence counts aggregated from the Open Scriptures Hebrew Bible / MorphHB:

- OSHB/MorphHB: <https://github.com/openscriptures/morphhb> (CC BY 4.0)

Where a vocabulary row records a source, `BDB` means Brown–Driver–Briggs (public domain)
and `OSHB` means Open Scriptures Hebrew Bible / MorphHB (CC BY 4.0). No content is copied
from any copyrighted course, workbook, or lexicon.
