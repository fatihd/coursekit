# Unit 56 — Reading, Review, and Final Checkpoint

Read a short passage and consolidate the whole course.

## Summary

Bring the course together by reading a short pointed passage aloud, parsing each word, and translating clause by clause. Apply the writing system, the noun and particle grammar, the verbs, and the syntax in order. Use this checkpoint to find which units still feel weak, then review them before finishing.

## Reading a passage

Work in a fixed order: read the words aloud, then parse each verb and identify each noun phrase and its relation, then translate clause by clause. Do not translate word by word; let each clause's structure — subject, verb, object, modifiers — determine the English.

## Reviewing the course

The most frequent words and the basic patterns carry most of the text, so the vocabulary you have learned should let you read a simple passage with a dictionary for the rest. Any part that still feels slow is the part to review: the writing system, the noun phrase, or the verb.

## Common confusion

Do not expect a passage to use only one form. Real Hebrew mixes Qatal and Yiqtol, nouns in the construct, pronouns, and particles all at once. Parse deliberately and trust the grammar; the goal is steady, accurate reading, not speed.

## Source

Davidson, *An Introductory Hebrew Grammar* (1914), §46.
