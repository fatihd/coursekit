# Unit 78 — Purpose Clauses

State purpose with לְמַעַן, פֶּן, and the infinitive.

## Summary

Purpose is stated with לְמַעַן (so that) or פֶּן (lest) followed by the imperfect, with an infinitive construct (לִשְׁמֹר, to keep), or with waw and a jussive or cohortative after a command.

## Purpose

לְמַעַן states a positive purpose, "so that": לְמַעַן תִּחְיֶה, "so that you may live". פֶּן states a negative purpose, "lest": פֶּן תִּשְׁכַּח, "lest you forget". These often follow an imperative or a command, giving the reason for it.

## Common confusion

Do not translate פֶּן as a plain "not": it states a feared result, "lest", and usually follows a warning or command.

## Source

Davidson, *An Introductory Hebrew Grammar* (22nd ed., 1923), §23, §49.
