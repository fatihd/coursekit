# Unit 20 — The Conjunction Waw

Join words and clauses with waw and read its changing vowel.

## Summary

The conjunction וְ (and) is written on the front of the next word. Its vowel changes with the sound that follows: וְ before most consonants; וּ before ב, מ, פ and before a shewa; וִ before יְ, as in וִיהוּדָה (and Judah); the matching short vowel before a composite shewa; and often וָ before a stressed syllable in pairs like יוֹם וָלַיְלָה (day and night).

## Waw

The conjunction ו attaches to the front of a word: דָּבָר (word) becomes וּדְבַר (and a word). Before a consonant its vowel is usually a vocal shewa (וְ), but before a shewa, a guttural, or a labial consonant it becomes û or takes another form. These small changes are pronunciation only; the meaning stays "and".

## Common confusion

Do not read ו as a full word with a fixed vowel. Its vowel adapts to the next sound, so וּ, וַ, וֶ, וֹ all occur as the same conjunction in different positions. Recognise the prefixed ו as "and" and let the following word determine how it is read.

## Source

Davidson, *An Introductory Hebrew Grammar* (22nd ed., 1923), §15.
