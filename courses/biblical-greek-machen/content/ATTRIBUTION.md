## Sources

- **J. Gresham Machen, *New Testament Greek for Beginners*** (New York: Macmillan, 1923). Public domain everywhere:
  published 1923 (its renewed US copyright ended on 2018-12-31) and Machen died on 1937-01-01. The course follows the
  text of the 1923 plates (January 1924 printing, archive.org `newtestamentgree00mach_0`); misprints are corrected from
  the 1927 corrected printing, each fix listed with its page in `build/_sources/machen/corrections.json` of the
  authoring hub. The unit order follows Machen's lessons; the exercise sentences are Machen's, transcribed by hand from
  the scans, and each links its scan page. Machen published no key: **every answer was written for this course**, and
  every Greek word form in the answers is attested in the SBLGNT (as analysed by MorphGNT), printed in Machen, or formed
  exactly on a paradigm Machen prints (each such form is listed with its page in the hub's
  `build/_sources/machen/attested.json`).
- **The lesson text** is newly written for this course; it is not a transcription of Machen's wording. Paradigm tables
  give standard Greek forms.
- **MorphGNT** (SBLGNT edition, github.com/morphgnt/sblgnt): parsing and lemmas, CC BY-SA 3.0, used for the NT
  frequencies in `content/vocab.csv` and to check the Greek of the answers.
- **SBLGNT** (Society of Biblical Literature Greek New Testament, CC BY 4.0) through MorphGNT.
- Vocabulary glosses follow Machen's lesson vocabularies (public domain).
