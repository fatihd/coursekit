# Unit 47 — Reading Vocabulary 14

Optional: New Testament words that occur 10 times.

## Summary

This optional unit adds 50 words that occur 10 times in the New Testament and are not in Machen's lessons: 19 nouns, 20 verbs and 11 other words. Nothing in the course waits for them; they are here so that you can read the New Testament with the lexicon open less often. Learn them on the Words track: recognize, recall, type, type with accents.

## Built from words you know

These verbs are a preposition plus a verb from the lessons; the meaning usually follows from the parts: ἐκπίπτω (πίπτω); παράγω (ἄγω).

## Common confusion

Look-alikes that differ by one letter: ἀφαιρέω (I take away) and ἀναιρέω (I take up); βίος (life) and υἱός (a son); ἰσχύς (strength) and ἰσχύω (I am strong); κλητός (called) and κλῆρος (a lot); κομίζω (I bring) and νομίζω (I think); ὄντως (really) and οὕτως (thus); ὅρκος (an oath) and οἶκος (a house). Feminine although they end in -ος, like ὁδός: βίβλος, κοινωνός.

## Source

Words chosen by their New Testament frequency (MorphGNT); glosses after Jeffrey Dodson's Greek-English lexicon (public domain).
