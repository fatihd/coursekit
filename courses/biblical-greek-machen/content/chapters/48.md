# Unit 48 — Reading Vocabulary 15

Optional: New Testament words that occur 10 times.

## Summary

This optional unit adds 24 words that occur 10 times in the New Testament and are not in Machen's lessons: 13 nouns, 8 verbs and 3 other words. Nothing in the course waits for them; they are here so that you can read the New Testament with the lexicon open less often. Learn them on the Words track: recognize, recall, type, type with accents.

## Common confusion

Look-alikes that differ by one letter: πύλη (a door) and φυλή (a tribe); χρυσός (gold) and χρυσοῦς (golden). Neuter nouns in -ος with the genitive -ους, like γένος: ψεῦδος.

## Source

Words chosen by their New Testament frequency (MorphGNT); glosses after Jeffrey Dodson's Greek-English lexicon (public domain).
