# Unit 36 — Reading Vocabulary 3

Optional: New Testament words that occur 25–29 times.

## Summary

This optional unit adds 50 words that occur 25–29 times in the New Testament and are not in Machen's lessons: 18 nouns, 22 verbs and 10 other words. Nothing in the course waits for them; they are here so that you can read the New Testament with the lexicon open less often. Learn them on the Words track: recognize, recall, type, type with accents.

## Built from words you know

These verbs are a preposition plus a verb from the lessons; the meaning usually follows from the parts: παρέρχομαι (ἔρχομαι).

## Common confusion

Look-alikes that differ by one letter: πάσχα (the feast of Passover) and πάσχω (I suffer); ποτέ (once) and τότε (then); πόσος (how much) and ὅσος (as great as); σταυρός (a cross) and σταυρόω (I crucify); ἐπεί (after) and ἐκεῖ (there); Μαριάμ (Mary) and Μαρία (Mary); χήρα (a widow) and χαρά (joy). Feminine although they end in -ος, like ὁδός: Αἴγυπτος. Neuter nouns in -ος with the genitive -ους, like γένος: ἔλεος.

## Source

Words chosen by their New Testament frequency (MorphGNT); glosses after Jeffrey Dodson's Greek-English lexicon (public domain).
