# Unit 21 — The Subjunctive

The subjunctive mood: purpose with ἵνα, future conditions with ἐάν, and the hortatory subjunctive.

## Summary

The subjunctive has only a present and an aorist (in the New Testament). It always takes primary endings, joined by the long variable vowel ω/η instead of ο/ε. The aorist subjunctive has no augment: it is made on the unaugmented aorist stem (λύσω, λίπω, λυθῶ). The tenses of the subjunctive differ not in time but in kind of action: the present for continuing or repeated action, the aorist for the action as a simple event. Its negative is μή.

## λύω, present subjunctive

| Person | Active | Middle and passive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1st sg. | λύω | λύωμαι |
| 2nd sg. | λύῃς | λύῃ |
| 3rd sg. | λύῃ | λύηται |
| 1st pl. | λύωμεν | λυώμεθα |
| 2nd pl. | λύητε | λύησθε |
| 3rd pl. | λύωσι(ν) | λύωνται |

## Aorist subjunctive

| Person | Active | Middle | Passive |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1st sg. | λύσω | λύσωμαι | λυθῶ |
| 2nd sg. | λύσῃς | λύσῃ | λυθῇς |
| 3rd sg. | λύσῃ | λύσηται | λυθῇ |
| 1st pl. | λύσωμεν | λυσώμεθα | λυθῶμεν |
| 2nd pl. | λύσητε | λύσησθε | λυθῆτε |
| 3rd pl. | λύσωσι(ν) | λύσωνται | λυθῶσι(ν) |

The second aorist subjunctive has the same endings on the second aorist stem: λίπω, λίπῃς, λίπῃ. The present subjunctive of εἰμί is ὦ, ᾖς, ᾖ, ὦμεν, ἦτε, ὦσι(ν).

## Uses

- Hortatory: the first person plural urges: πιστεύσωμεν, let us believe; μὴ ἁμαρτάνωμεν, let us not sin.
- Purpose: ἵνα with the subjunctive, in order that: ἦλθεν ἵνα σώσῃ ἁμαρτωλούς.
- Future condition: ἐάν with the subjunctive, if (in future time): ἐὰν ἴδῃ τὸν κύριον, πιστεύσει. A condition about the present or past takes εἰ with the indicative: εἰ ἐλάβομεν ταύτην τὴν ἐντολήν.

## Common confusion

λύσω is both the aorist subjunctive (that I may loose) and the future indicative (I shall loose); λύσῃ is aorist subjunctive active third singular or future middle second singular. The construction (ἵνα, ἐάν, or a main verb) decides.
