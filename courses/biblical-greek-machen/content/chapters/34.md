# Unit 34 — Reading Vocabulary 1

Optional: New Testament words that occur 36–49 times.

## Summary

This optional unit adds 50 words that occur 36–49 times in the New Testament and are not in Machen's lessons: 19 nouns, 21 verbs and 10 other words. Nothing in the course waits for them; they are here so that you can read the New Testament with the lexicon open less often. Learn them on the Words track: recognize, recall, type, type with accents.

## Built from words you know

These verbs are a preposition plus a verb from the lessons; the meaning usually follows from the parts: ἐπιγινώσκω (γινώσκω); παρίστημι (ἵστημι).

## Common confusion

Look-alikes that differ by one letter: ναός (a temple) and λαός (a people); καινός (fresh) and καιρός (a time); χωρίς (apart from) and χάρις (grace); ἄξιος (worthy) and ἅγιος (holy); σήμερον (today) and σημεῖον (a sign); δικαιόω (I make righteous) and δίκαιος (righteous); ἄρτι (now) and ἀντί (instead of). Neuter nouns in -ος with the genitive -ους, like γένος: μέρος, τέλος.

## Source

Words chosen by their New Testament frequency (MorphGNT); glosses after Jeffrey Dodson's Greek-English lexicon (public domain).
