# Unit 42 — Reading Vocabulary 9

Optional: New Testament words that occur 14–15 times.

## Summary

This optional unit adds 50 words that occur 14–15 times in the New Testament and are not in Machen's lessons: 13 nouns, 24 verbs and 13 other words. Nothing in the course waits for them; they are here so that you can read the New Testament with the lexicon open less often. Learn them on the Words track: recognize, recall, type, type with accents.

## Built from words you know

These verbs are a preposition plus a verb from the lessons; the meaning usually follows from the parts: συνίστημι (ἵστημι); ἀνθίστημι (ἵστημι); ἐξαποστέλλω (ἀποστέλλω); κατεσθίω (ἐσθίω).

## Common confusion

Look-alikes that differ by one letter: τύπος (a figure) and τόπος (a place); ἀναγγέλλω (I announce) and ἀπαγγέλλω (I report); ἀνθίστημι (I take a stand against) and ἀνίστημι (I cause to rise); ἅπαξ (once) and ἅπας (all); ἕκτος (sixth) and ἔτος (a year); κλάω (I break) and κλαίω (I weep); κοινός (common) and καινός (fresh); κοινόω (I make unclean) and κοινός (common).

## Source

Words chosen by their New Testament frequency (MorphGNT); glosses after Jeffrey Dodson's Greek-English lexicon (public domain).
