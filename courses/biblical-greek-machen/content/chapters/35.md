# Unit 35 — Reading Vocabulary 2

Optional: New Testament words that occur 29–36 times.

## Summary

This optional unit adds 50 words that occur 29–36 times in the New Testament and are not in Machen's lessons: 26 nouns, 14 verbs and 10 other words. Nothing in the course waits for them; they are here so that you can read the New Testament with the lexicon open less often. Learn them on the Words track: recognize, recall, type, type with accents.

## Built from words you know

These verbs are a preposition plus a verb from the lessons; the meaning usually follows from the parts: ἀναγινώσκω (γινώσκω); ἐπικαλέω (καλέω); ἐπιτιμάω (τιμάω).

## Common confusion

Look-alikes that differ by one letter: μέλος (a limb) and μέρος (a part); μήτε (nor) and μηδέ (and not); οἶνος (wine) and οἶκος (a house); ὁμοίως (in like manner) and ὅμοιος (like); φαίνω (I shine) and βαίνω (I go); Ἠλίας (Elijah) and ἥλιος (the sun). Neuter nouns in -ος with the genitive -ους, like γένος: μέλος.

## Source

Words chosen by their New Testament frequency (MorphGNT); glosses after Jeffrey Dodson's Greek-English lexicon (public domain).
