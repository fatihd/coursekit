# Unit 13 — The Future

The future active and middle indicative, the future of stems ending in a consonant, and verbs with a deponent future.

## Summary

The future is made on the future stem: the verb stem plus σ (λυ- + σ = λυσ-), with the primary endings joined by the variable vowel. A labial (π, β, φ) and σ make ψ (πέμψω); a palatal (κ, γ, χ) and σ make ξ (ἄξω, διώξω); a dental (τ, δ, θ) drops before σ (πείσω, δοξάσω). Many verbs have a different stem in the present: κηρύσσω has κηρυκ-, future κηρύξω; διδάσκω has διδαχ-, future διδάξω. Learn each verb's future with the verb.

## λύσω, I shall loose

| Person | Singular | Plural |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1st | λύσω | λύσομεν |
| 2nd | λύσεις | λύσετε |
| 3rd | λύσει | λύσουσι(ν) |

## λύσομαι, I shall loose for myself

| Person | Singular | Plural |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1st | λύσομαι | λυσόμεθα |
| 2nd | λύσῃ | λύσεσθε |
| 3rd | λύσεται | λύσονται |

## Deponent futures

Some verbs that are active in the present have a middle future with an active meaning: γινώσκω, γνώσομαι; λαμβάνω, λήμψομαι; βαίνω, βήσομαι; and ἔρχομαι has ἐλεύσομαι, γίνομαι has γενήσομαι. ἔχω has the future ἕξω, with a rough breathing.

## Common confusion

λύσεις (you will loose) and λύεις (you loose) differ only by σ; λύσει (he will loose) and λύσῃ (you will loose for yourself) differ in the last vowel.
