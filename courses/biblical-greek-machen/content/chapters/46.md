# Unit 46 — Reading Vocabulary 13

Optional: New Testament words that occur 10–11 times.

## Summary

This optional unit adds 50 words that occur 10–11 times in the New Testament and are not in Machen's lessons: 22 nouns, 15 verbs and 13 other words. Nothing in the course waits for them; they are here so that you can read the New Testament with the lexicon open less often. Learn them on the Words track: recognize, recall, type, type with accents.

## Built from words you know

These verbs are a preposition plus a verb from the lessons; the meaning usually follows from the parts: ἀναφέρω (φέρω); ἀπολαμβάνω (λαμβάνω).

## Common confusion

Look-alikes that differ by one letter: κέρας (a horn) and τέρας (a wonder); μακρός (long) and μικρός (small); νόσος (a disease) and νόμος (a law); πηγή (a fountain) and πληγή (a blow); Τύρος (Tyre) and τύπος (a figure); ἀδύνατος (incapable) and δυνατός (powerful). Feminine although they end in -ος, like ὁδός: νόσος, Τύρος.

## Source

Words chosen by their New Testament frequency (MorphGNT); glosses after Jeffrey Dodson's Greek-English lexicon (public domain).
