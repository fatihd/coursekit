# Unit 45 — Reading Vocabulary 12

Optional: New Testament words that occur 11–12 times.

## Summary

This optional unit adds 50 words that occur 11–12 times in the New Testament and are not in Machen's lessons: 21 nouns, 23 verbs and 6 other words. Nothing in the course waits for them; they are here so that you can read the New Testament with the lexicon open less often. Learn them on the Words track: recognize, recall, type, type with accents.

## Built from words you know

These verbs are a preposition plus a verb from the lessons; the meaning usually follows from the parts: συνέχω (ἔχω); ἀντιλέγω (λέγω); εἰσάγω (ἄγω); ἐμβλέπω (βλέπω); ἐνδείκνυμι (δείκνυμι); ἐπιπίπτω (πίπτω).

## Common confusion

Look-alikes that differ by one letter: τρίς (three times) and τρεῖς (three); δόλος (deceit) and δοῦλος (a slave); ἔλαιον (olive oil) and ἔλαβον (I took); ζηλόω (I am jealous) and δηλόω (I show); θανατόω (I put to death) and θάνατος (a death); θάπτω (I bury) and ἅπτω (I light).

## Source

Words chosen by their New Testament frequency (MorphGNT); glosses after Jeffrey Dodson's Greek-English lexicon (public domain).
