# Unit 37 — Reading Vocabulary 4

Optional: New Testament words that occur 22–25 times.

## Summary

This optional unit adds 50 words that occur 22–25 times in the New Testament and are not in Machen's lessons: 19 nouns, 23 verbs and 8 other words. Nothing in the course waits for them; they are here so that you can read the New Testament with the lexicon open less often. Learn them on the Words track: recognize, recall, type, type with accents.

## Built from words you know

These verbs are a preposition plus a verb from the lessons; the meaning usually follows from the parts: πάρειμι (εἰμί); προσέχω (ἔχω); ἀνάγω (ἄγω); καταλείπω (λείπω); περιβάλλω (βάλλω).

## Common confusion

Look-alikes that differ by one letter: νοῦς (the mind) and πούς (a foot); ἄπιστος (unbelieving) and πιστός (faithful); εἰκών (an image) and εἰπών (having said); νέος (young) and θεός (a god). Neuter nouns in -ος with the genitive -ους, like γένος: σκεῦος.

## Source

Words chosen by their New Testament frequency (MorphGNT); glosses after Jeffrey Dodson's Greek-English lexicon (public domain).
